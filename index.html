How it works

  • Add your files. Free use covers 2 compressions a day.
  • Pick a quality level. “Visually lossless” is smaller and looks identical to the eye.
  • Download. If a result isn’t smaller, we keep your original.

Lossless mode keeps every pixel. Photos and video are already compressed, so most savings come from re-encoding that your eyes can’t distinguish. Video is processed in real time in your browser. For frame-exact lossless video, use a desktop tool such as FFmpeg.

Popular uses

Compress an image to 100KB for an online application, reduce photo size for WhatsApp or email, convert PNG or JPG to WebP for a faster website, or shrink a video to fit an upload limit.

FAQ

Are my files uploaded?

No. Compression runs inside your browser. Your files stay on your device.

Is it really lossless?

Lossless mode keeps every pixel identical. Visually lossless mode changes data your eyes can’t notice and gives smaller files.

Why is video slow?

Your browser re-records the video in real time, so a 1-minute clip takes about a minute. Keep the tab open.

What counts as a compression?

Each file you compress counts once. The free limit resets daily.

How do I unlock more?

Watch a short ad for 3 more compressions, or subscribe to Pro for unlimited use without ads.
